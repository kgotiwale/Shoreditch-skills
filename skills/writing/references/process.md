# Writing Clearly and Concisely: Process Detail

Strunk's *The Elements of Style* (1918), split into loadable sections, plus the AI-pattern field guide. Load only the section the task needs; see the map at the foot of this file.

## Strunk's 18 rules (full listing)

### Grammar and punctuation (Rules 1-7)

1. Form the possessive singular of nouns by adding 's.
2. In a series of three or more terms with a single conjunction, use a comma after each term except the last (the Oxford comma).
3. Enclose parenthetic expressions between commas.
4. Place a comma before a conjunction introducing a coordinate clause.
5. Do not join independent clauses with a comma alone.
6. Do not break sentences in two.
7. A participial phrase at the beginning of a sentence must refer to the grammatical subject.

### Composition (Rules 8-18)

8. Make the paragraph the unit of composition: one paragraph to each topic.
9. As a rule, begin each paragraph with a topic sentence.
10. Use the active voice: "He repaired the car", not "The car was repaired by him".
11. Put statements in positive form: "dishonest", not "not honest".
12. Use definite, specific, concrete language. Avoid vague generalities.
13. Omit needless words: "the question as to whether" becomes "whether".
14. Avoid a succession of loose sentences.
15. Express coordinate ideas in similar form (parallelism).
16. Keep related words together. Do not split subject from verb without reason.
17. In summaries, keep to one tense.
18. Place the emphatic words of a sentence at the end.
